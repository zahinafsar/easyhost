# What is a platform as a service?

A platform as a service (PaaS) runs your application for you.

## The short version

A platform as a service (PaaS) runs your application for you. You provide the code, and the platform handles servers, builds, networking and HTTPS. You trade some control over the machine for much less operations work. It sits between raw virtual machines, where you manage everything, and serverless functions, where you manage only handlers.
