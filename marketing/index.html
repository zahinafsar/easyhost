How it works

From folder to live URL in three steps.

  1. 1

    Create an account

    Sign up in the dashboard. It takes less than a minute.

  2. 2

    Log in from your terminal

    Run npx ezhst login inside your Next.js project.

  3. 3

    Deploy

    Run npx ezhst deploy. We install, build and start your app, then hand you the URL.
