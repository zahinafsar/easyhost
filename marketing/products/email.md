# Transactional email (coming soon)

Transactional email for your apps is coming soon. It is not available yet.

## What you get

- The dashboard Email page shows Coming soon
- The `ezhst email` commands and the email API return `Email is coming soon.`
- Account emails from EasyHost itself, such as verification codes, password resets and invites, are still sent
- Until it launches, send app email through a provider of your choice and keep its key in an env var

## Try it

There is nothing to set up yet. Store your email provider's key with `ezhst env set` and run `ezhst redeploy`.
