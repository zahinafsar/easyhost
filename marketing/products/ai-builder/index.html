AI Builder

Describe an app in the dashboard and AI builds the frontend and the backend, live at <name>.easyhost.fun while you chat.

Last updated November 9, 2026

What you get

  • Plain JavaScript and Hono, running as a real Node.js app
  • The AI asks a few questions, shows a short plan to approve, then lets you pick one of 9 themes
  • Uses your project's Postgres and storage when the app needs them (email is coming soon)
  • Every step is shown as it happens: files written, SQL run, packages installed

Try it

  1. Open Builder in the dashboard and create an app. The name becomes its address.
  2. Describe what it should do.
  3. Answer the questions, approve the plan and pick a theme. The app goes live as the AI builds it.

Limits

Your first AI app and its first build are free. After that, AI requests use AI Tokens, bought once and never expiring: 500 tokens for $5, 1,100 for $10, 2,400 for $20 or 6,500 for $50. Each request is charged by how much work it takes, and a typical full build is a few hundred tokens. Hosting the app stays free. 30 AI prompts per day per project, and AI apps count toward the 3 apps per project. The AI Builder is in the dashboard only, not the CLI. See pricing and limits for everything.
