# Team members

Invite people to a project by email. Members manage everything in it; only the owner can invite, remove, rename or delete.

## What you get

- Email invites that work for people without an account
- Members manage apps, deploys, storage, database and schedules
- The owner can revoke invites and remove members at any time
- Members can leave a project themselves

## Try it

```
ezhst members invite teammate@example.com
ezhst members list
ezhst accept-invite <link-or-token>
```

## Limits

10 members and pending invites per project. Invite links expire after 7 days. See [pricing and limits](/pricing) for everything.
