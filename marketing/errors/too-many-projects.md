# You can have at most 3 projects

Your account already has three projects, which is the limit for every account.

## The error

```
You can have at most 3 projects.
```

## Why it happens

Your account already has three projects, which is the limit for every account.

## How to fix it

Delete a project you no longer need, or add the app to an existing project, which can hold up to three apps.

```
ezhst projects list
ezhst projects delete --project old-project --yes
```
