# Domain is an EasyHost domain and can't be added

Addresses under `easyhost.fun` are managed by the platform, and your app already has one.

## The error

```
my-app.easyhost.fun is an EasyHost domain and can't be added
```

## Why it happens

Addresses under `easyhost.fun` are managed by the platform, and your app already has one.

## How to fix it

Use the URL you already have, or add a domain you own.

```
ezhst domains add www.yourdomain.com
```
