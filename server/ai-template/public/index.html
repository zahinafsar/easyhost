Your AI app is live

This page is running on EasyHost, a developer hosting platform with apps, Postgres databases, file storage and email in one place.

How to build your app

  1. Open this app in the EasyHost Builder.
  2. Describe what you want, for example "a todo list with sign up and log in".
  3. The AI writes the code and the changes show up here within seconds.
  4. Keep chatting to change, fix or extend it.

What your app can use

  • A Postgres database for your data
  • File storage for uploads and images
  • Email sending from your app's own address
  • Sign up and log in for your users
